Accessibility

Last updated September 10, 2026

We want everyone to be able to find our games, tournament details, hours, and ways to visit. If something on this website creates a barrier, please let us know so we can help.

Get help or report a problem

Email, call, or text us if you need website information in another format or have trouble using a feature.

contact@eclipsepinball.com
Call 804-420-2188 · Text 804-420-2188

It helps to include the page address, what you were trying to do, and how you would like us to contact you. If relevant, you can also tell us which browser or assistive technology you use.

Our approach

We aim to improve this website using the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA as a reference. A comprehensive accessibility audit has not yet been completed, and we do not claim full conformance.

The website includes:

  • Keyboard focus indicators and links to skip to the main content or game list.
  • Labeled game search, sorting, and filter controls, with a text result count.
  • Text for the address, hours, admission price, and featured tournament details.
  • Support for your device’s reduced-motion preference to stop decorative animations.
  • Page layouts and text sizes that adapt to different screen sizes and browser font settings.

Current limitations and alternatives

The embedded Google Map and linked social or tournament websites are provided by third parties and may present accessibility barriers. You can find our address and hours as text in the Visit section, or contact us for assistance.

The interactive game list needs JavaScript. If it does not load or is difficult to use, you can view the listing on Pinball Map or ask us for lineup information by email, phone, or text.

Planning a visit

For questions about access or assistance at the arcade, contact us before your visit. We are at 1310 Altamont Avenue, Richmond, VA 23230. Our current hours and contact details are in the Visit section.
