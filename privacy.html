Privacy

Last updated September 10, 2026

This notice describes information involved when you visit eclipsepinball.com or contact Eclipse Pinball using the links on this website.

Information you share with us

If you email, call, or text us, we receive the contact information and message you choose to share. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and discuss arrangements you request, such as a private event. The email and phone service providers used by you and Eclipse also handle those communications.

The website does not offer account registration, online checkout, or a contact form. Its game search and filters run in your browser; those searches are not sent to Eclipse Pinball.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. Learn more in GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection.

Google Maps and cookies

The Visit section includes an embedded Google Map. When the map loads, your browser connects to Google. Google may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device details, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its services and your settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking third-party cookies or content may affect the map. Our address and hours are also available as text in the Visit section.

Eclipse has not added advertising pixels, visitor analytics, or its own tracking cookies to this website.

Photos, game listings, and other websites

The fonts, game roster, and photos shown in the Instagram section are served with this website. Viewing those photos does not load an Instagram feed or tracking script.

Links to Instagram, Facebook, Pinball Map, tournament calendars, and map services take you to other services. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Questions or requests

For questions about this notice or information you have shared with us, including requests to access, correct, or delete it, contact Eclipse Pinball. You can also ask how long a particular inquiry or event record is kept. Applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements may affect a request.
