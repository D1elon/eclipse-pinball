      Good to know

      Common Questions

      The things people ask us most. Anything not covered here, just call, text, or email. We'd rather answer it than have you guess.

      How does it work?

      One price, all day. $15 gets you in and every machine is set to free play until close. No quarters, no tokens, nothing to load up. Step out and come back the same day at no extra charge.

      Why do the playfields look so clear?

      Every game on the floor has anti-reflective glass. It kills the glare from the lights overhead, so you can actually see what you're shooting at instead of your own reflection. Very few arcades anywhere have it across the whole lineup. If you've only played on standard glass, you'll notice it on the first ball.

      Can I play multiplayer with friends?

      Yes. Press the credit button once for each player before you start, and the game sets up a multiplayer match. Up to four on most machines.

      Do you sell food or drinks?

      We have a small selection of non-alcoholic drinks. Food and alcohol are coming in the fall/winter.

      How do I report a problem with a game?

      Scan the QR code on the machine, or flag down an arcade attendant. Either works. We'd rather know, so tell us even if it seems minor.

      Does my entry cover DawnStar's arcade?

      Entry is separate for each. Re-entry is always welcome at both, though, and plenty of people spend the day going back and forth.

      How do I book a private event?

      Email contact@eclipsepinball.com and we'll work out the details: birthdays, work nights out, or the whole floor to yourselves.
